Shell and navigation

AppShell is the room everything else stands in. It renders the hamburger toggle, the Sidebar, and the #main-content region wrapped in an ErrorBoundary, and mounts four deferred passengers: EngagementLayer, KeyboardShortcuts, PreferenceModal, and ThingsComingModal. It owns two first-run decisions — showing the product tour when reallearn-things-coming-seen is absent, and the preference modal for signed-in users who have not completed onboarding — and two hygiene behaviors: the sidebar closes on every route change, and while it is open the body scroll locks and Escape closes it. On /sign-in and /sign-up the shell collapses to just the content region, keeping the skip-link target alive on auth pages.

The Sidebar (403 lines) is the app's memory made visible. Its header holds the RL brand block and a full-width "＋ New lesson" .btn-primary; its body lists saved lessons from savedJourneysStore, with a search field that appears once more than four journeys exist and filters case- and accent-insensitively (comments note that scanning up to 100 saved lessons by scrolling is slow). Opening an entry calls getArchivedLesson() for a free IndexedDB read before ever considering regeneration. Deletion routes through the lazily imported ConfirmModal, and the theme picker through the lazily imported ThemeModal — both excluded from the initial bundle because they only matter on click. Navbar stays deliberately small: brand, three links (Home, Learn, Progress) with active-state classes, and ProgressHub, the memoized widget that compresses the whole engagement system into a flame emoji, a level ring, and a goal count. Footer adds the quiet "Learning together for N days" companion counter, with special copy at the 7, 30, 100, and 365-day milestones.

System states: loading, error, and announcement

The repository's UX principles require a real UI for every async state, and a dedicated component exists for each. LoadingCinematic is the flagship: while a lesson generates it shows a percentage that blends real SSE progress with an ease-out auto-completion curve (constant AUTO_COMPLETE_DURATION_MS = 3500, because fast-mode generations can finish in two seconds with only a handful of progress events), a six-step checklist whose thresholds are spread from 5% to 95% so no two steps light at once, rotating study facts, a skeleton preview of the three part cards, a cancel control, and a reassurance line after thirty seconds. ErrorState and ErrorBoundary split responsibilities between expected failures (a friendly card with retry) and render crashes (a class-component catch with reset). ToastContainer exposes a global showToast() that dispatches a reallearn:toast CustomEvent; the container keys its 3.2-second dismissal timer to the head toast only — a comment records that keying on the whole array let a burst of toasts pin the oldest one on screen indefinitely — and its aria-live wrapper stays permanently mounted so screen readers announce the first toast after idle. LiveRegion and SkipToContent round out the accessibility scaffolding, and Skeleton supplies the four placeholder primitives used across every page's hydration gate.

Consent and onboarding: the modal population

Six of the thirty-seven components are modals concerned with consent or first-run setup, and their coexistence is managed explicitly. lib/modalManager.ts provides a module-level mutual-exclusion lock (requestModal/releaseModal) because, as its docblock notes, several blocking modals share the same z-index and two could otherwise render simultaneously. PreSignInConsent — at 754 lines the largest component in the codebase — is the legal gate: it summarizes policy changes (six privacy bullets, three terms bullets in the current version), collects a date of birth that is immediately reduced to an age bracket (under13, 13-17, 18+) for data minimization, restricts declined users to /sign-in, /sign-up, and /legal, and syncs acceptance to the backend. CookieConsent is separately versioned (CURRENT_COOKIE_VERSION = "2.3") and treats the server-side record as truth for signed-in users, mirroring it down into localStorage; declining after a previous accept dispatches COOKIE_CONSENT_REVOKED_EVENT, which GoogleAnalytics answers by setting GA's documented opt-out flag and deleting first-party _ga* cookies. GoogleAnalytics also validates NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID against /^[A-Za-z0-9_-]+$/ before interpolating it into a script tag — a script-injection guard called out in its comments. PersonalizationGate, PreferenceModal, and ThingsComingModal handle the friendlier half of onboarding: learning-style preferences, first-run defaults, and a four-step tour.

Engagement and delight

EngagementLayer (505 lines) is the renderer for progressStore's celebration queue. Each celebration kind has a tuned display duration — XP chips at 800 ms, level-ups and badges at 2,500 ms, streaks and daily goals at 2,000 ms — values a comment says were reduced so celebrations feel like "quick, delightful feedback, not blocking overlays". XP renders as a floating chip near the top of the viewport; the bigger moments get a card with a sixteen-piece particle burst. AchievementsGrid displays all badges from lib/achievements.ts with earned ones lit, and renders its explanatory popover through a portal into document.body — a forty-line comment documents why: the grid's ancestor uses content-visibility: auto, whose paint containment turns the section into the containing block for position: fixed, so an in-tree popover appeared far from the badge it described. ActivityHeatmap anchors its columns to the Saturday that ends the current week so each column is a true Sun–Sat calendar week, with future days as invisible spacers. EasterEggs hides four unannounced delights — Konami-code confetti, typed secret words, the footer heart burst, and once-per-day time greetings — all rendered pointer-events-none, with a localStorage pruning loop so its daily markers never accumulate.

Events as the shared bus

The shared layer coordinates through DOM CustomEvents rather than a global event library: reallearn:toast (toasts), reallearn:egg (footer to EasterEggs), reallearn:fillFollowUp (completion screen to FollowUpBox), reallearn:open-things-coming (re-opening the tour), and the three cookie-consent events defined in lib/legalConsent.ts. The pattern keeps producers and consumers decoupled across lazy-loaded chunk boundaries — a deferred provider can listen for events fired by code that loaded long before it did.

Small components, sharp edges

Even the smallest entries in this catalog carry documented defensive decisions. SourceTag treats every source URL from AI-generated content as untrusted, parsing it with new URL() and rendering only http(s) schemes as links — its comment names the alternative plainly: a javascript: URI here would be stored XSS. ConfirmModal guards the Enter key so that pressing Enter while focused on Cancel cannot trigger the destructive confirm. ThemeApplier keeps the mobile address bar tinted to the theme's page background rather than the picker swatch, fixing a bug where the browser chrome once painted flag-orange. ReadingProgressBar clamps its scroll ratio to [0,1] because iOS rubber-banding overshoots both ends. None of these behaviors are visible in a screenshot; all of them are visible in a bug tracker that stays empty.
